Chapter 1

Hello, hello, my little dreamers! Gather 'round and listen close, for I have a wonderful story about a very special boy named Leo and his incredible invention.



Chapter 2

Leo was a boy with a head full of gears and a heart full of dreams. His favorite place was the cluttered shed behind his house, smelling of sawdust and imagination. For weeks, he’d been secretly working on his most ambitious invention yet. It looked like a bicycle, with shiny chrome handlebars and pedals ready for pumping. But if you looked closely, you'd spot tiny, tucked-away wings and a peculiar whirring device beneath the seat. "Just a few more adjustments," Leo whispered to his contraption, "and we'll be ready for takeoff!" He couldn't wait to soar.



Chapter 3

The next morning, before the sun had fully stretched across the sky, Leo wheeled his creation to the highest hill overlooking his town. He climbed aboard, heart thumping like a drum. He pedaled hard, faster and faster, until the tiny wings fluttered and the whirring device hummed to life. With a gentle bump, the wheels left the grass! Leo was flying! The houses below looked like toy blocks, and the trees were tiny green broccoli florets. He laughed, a joyful sound carried on the wind, as he steered his magnificent flying bicycle higher and higher into the crisp morning air.
